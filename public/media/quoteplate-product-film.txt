Let’s make a first purchase with Monsoon Table, our example restaurant.

Start with your restaurant’s name, contact number, and address. Add your city, state and PIN code for the right delivery details.

Enter the owner’s name and work email. For approved pilot owners, continue with Google to create the workspace and open Today.

Open Menu, choose Add menu, then type or paste a dish. Here, we’re making tomato curry. Review the ingredient, quantity and unit. Approve the menu before using it for a purchase.

Open Suppliers and add the businesses you buy from. Enter each business name and its ingredient categories. We’ve added two example suppliers so we can compare their replies.

Choose New purchase. Give it a name and select the approved menu. Check quantities. We need ten kilos of tomatoes. Set the delivery date and reply deadline. Choose your suppliers, save the draft, then open it for quotes.

Each supplier opens their private request link. They enter a price, check delivery and the total, then send their quote. Here are replies from our two example suppliers, for the same ten kilos.

Back in Purchases, compare the complete offers. Check coverage, tax, delivery charges and dates. Here, the totals are four hundred and four hundred forty rupees. Choose the suitable supplier and confirm your decision.

Now check what actually arrives. We ordered ten kilos, but received nine. Enter received quantities and check the invoice. Record the missing kilo and forty rupees in credit still owed, then save.

Return to Today. Your purchase and delivery follow-up are visible. Your team can see what needs attention next.
