# Clear restaurant and vendor interface

The user approved five main restaurant destinations and one clear buying sequence, then requested the same clarity for vendors who struggle with technology. Every current feature must remain available.

Use the existing restrained QuotePlate colors, consistent sans-serif UI, clear headings, quiet section separators and 44px touch targets. Primary navigation: Today, Purchases, Suppliers, Menu, Reports. Settings is a utility. Preserve URLs and group meal planning, purchase history, supplier conversations and delivery reports under contextual links.

Purchases follow Choose ingredients → Compare prices → Choose supplier → Check delivery. Give each state its next action. Make exports, technical evidence and optional trade details clearly labelled disclosures. Never hide errors, required inputs, privacy controls, or change quote/order semantics. Preserve drafts, revisions, split awards, credits and every existing capability.

Put saved suppliers before discovery. Show reports as concise outcomes with optional explanations. Planning separates meals/portions, stock and shortages; preserve explicit quantities/yields and menu review/approval. Vendor pages prioritize entering prices, reviewing totals and sending quotes, then confirming orders and deliveries; optional business settings/history remain reachable.

UI only: no schema or pricing arithmetic changes, no external messages, no new dependencies or paid services, no fabricated live data, no public internal-demo promotion. Keep the public landing/video unchanged for this scope. Validate all existing workflows, grouped navigation, keyboard access and error disclosure, mobile/desktop visuals and no page overflow. Full CI precedes the already-authorized PR merge and publish.
